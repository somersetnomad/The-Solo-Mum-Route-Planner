Three questions everyone asks

"Which clinic is cheapest?"

The price per cycle is rarely the whole bill. Donor sperm, drugs, tests, scans and storage are where the surprises are.

"Will the NHS help me?"

It depends on your postcode. Many areas ask single women to pay for 6–12 attempts at insemination first.

"Can I just use a donor at home?"

You can, but outside a licensed clinic the donor can be your child's legal father. The rules matter before you start.

Questions

Who is it for?

Single women in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland who are thinking about, or starting, donor conception. Most of it applies whatever your age or stage.

What format is it?

A 20-page A4 PDF. You go straight to the download after paying. Print it, or fill it in with any PDF editor.

How current is the information?

Figures were checked in October 2026 against the HFEA, NICE (guideline NG257, March 2026), NHS and GOV.UK sources, all listed at the back. Prices and local NHS rules change, so the workbook shows you how to check your own.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. It is general information to help you plan and ask better questions. Please talk to your GP, a licensed fertility clinic and, for any known-donor arrangement, a family solicitor.
